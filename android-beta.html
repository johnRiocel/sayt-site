Sayt for Android is in closed testing on Google Play. Leave your email and you'll get an invite link as soon as a build is ready to test.

Sign up with your email

Takes ten seconds · Just an email address · No account needed

Why sign up

Google Play requires a tester list before you can install

Closed testing on Google Play only lets in testers whose email is on the list, and only once they've accepted an invite. Signing up here adds your email to that list, so you can install the app the moment a build goes out.

You won't get spam. The email is used only to send your Play Store invite link and, occasionally, a heads-up when a new build is ready to try.

What to expect

Same app, still being finished

  • Camera, observations, issues and plan pins already work
  • Cross-platform handoff with the iPhone version — a .saytpack file opens on either
  • Some screens are still catching up to the iOS build
  • Expect occasional rough edges — that's what testing is for

Feedback from testing shapes what gets fixed before the public Play Store release.
