Tutorial

Your first site visit, start to finish

Six steps, real screenshots and a real generated report — the same Camaya Residence visit used throughout this site. Nothing here is a mockup: every screen and every report page is what Sayt actually produces.

Version 1.1 · TestFlight beta · Last updated 13 September 2026

Before you start. The first time you open Sayt, pick the role that matches your work — civil engineer, QA/QC checker, architect, inspector and 14 others are available. This changes the finding categories and the wording of the report to match, so the rest of the app already speaks your language. See the full list in the documentation, §4.11.

Step 1 of 6

Start a visit

Create a project once — the address, the client, your reference number — then start a visit each time you're on site. The home screen opens on a calendar marking every day you've documented, so a job reads back as a storyboard rather than a folder of loose photos.

Set up a repeating schedule — daily, weekly, fortnightly or monthly — and Sayt reminds you before you're due back.

  • One project per site, unlimited visits inside it
  • Marked days for visits already documented
  • A local reminder before the next scheduled visit

Step 2 of 6

Photograph and measure a finding

Photograph what you're documenting, describe it and set a category and severity — that's the finding. Then tap Measure and point the camera at it: set the two ends and Sayt reads the distance straight off the camera, a ceiling height or a crack length, and writes it onto the finding.

Chain several lines around an opening or a patch of damage and close the shape to get the enclosed area — "how much render needs replacing?" answered on site, not estimated later.

  • Lengths and enclosed areas, both saved to the finding
  • On iPhones with LiDAR, ranges against real geometry for noticeably better accuracy
  • Metric or imperial, switchable at any time
  • Every measurement carries straight into the report

Step 3 of 6

Read the ruler on the wall

While you measure, Sayt draws a graduated ruler along whatever you're pointing at, numbered like a real tape. Each side of a chained shape keeps its own length, so a window comes back as four figures rather than one.

It also tells you what it has locked onto — Vertical surface or Horizontal surface — so you know the reading is coming off the wall you meant.

  • Graduated ticks and numbers along every line
  • Each side of a shape labelled with its own length
  • Tap a value to flip between metric and imperial
  • Every figure marked ≈ — an estimate, and it says so

Camera measurements are digital estimates. They're accurate enough to size an opening or brief a trade, but check critical dimensions with a tape before ordering material.

Step 4 of 6

Pin it to the plan

Import the floor plan — a PDF page or a photo of one — and tap it to drop a pin exactly where the finding is. The reader stops guessing which of four identical units, or which end of a corridor, the crack is in.

Pins are repositionable until you save, so getting it right takes a second. The plan and the pin both carry through to the exported report, next to the photo and its measurements.

  • Use any plan you can get onto the phone
  • One pin per finding, placed by tapping the plan
  • Plan and pin both appear in the exported report

Step 5 of 6

Preview before you send it

Before you generate anything, preview the finished pages — your company name, the project details, every measurement and observation already laid out. This is the real preview screen, not a mockup, from the same Camaya Residence visit used throughout this site.

Catch a typo, re-order a finding or add one more photo while it's still just a preview, rather than after a client already has the PDF.

  • Every page laid out exactly as it will export
  • Your company name, logo and licence number already on it
  • Nothing sent until you tap Generate

Step 6 of 6

Generate, and choose a template

Everything you recorded on site is already report-shaped, so generating the document is one screen and one tap. Choose the format — PDF, Word (DOCX) or the original photos as a ZIP — and the template that matches who's going to read it.

  • Preview it one more time before you generate
  • Toggle your branding on or off
  • Send it before you get back to the car
